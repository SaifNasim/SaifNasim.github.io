About me

I am a Computer Science graduate of Independent University, Bangladesh and a merit scholarship recipient, passionate about AI research, computer vision, and recent advanced models. My research focuses on improving Vision-Language Models and other downstream tasks in computer vision.

For my undergraduate thesis I studied how Bangladesh’s private economic zones changed between 2018 and 2025, using satellite land use/land cover (LULC), nighttime light (NTL), and population data. It was supervised by Dr. AKM Mahbubur Rahman (Head of the CSE Department, IUB) and Dr. Amin Masud Ali (Professor, Jahangirnagar University).

At CCDS.AI I worked with other research assistants and took part in weekly paper presentations. Among my course projects is a Vision-Language Model for remote sensing segmentation that combines CLIP with a CNN-based U-Net.

  • Spatio-Temporal Analysis and Urbanization Trajectories of Private Economic Zones in Bangladesh Using Remote Sensing Undergraduate thesis, Dec 2025 – Aug 2026
